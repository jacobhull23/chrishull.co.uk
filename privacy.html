Privacy

This is my own website. It doesn’t sell anything online, doesn’t use analytics or advertising, and doesn’t set any cookies. This page explains the little personal information it does handle.

The enquiry form

If you send a message through the contact form, your name, email address, the painting you’re interested in and your message are delivered to my email by FormSubmit, a form-delivery service based in the United States.

I use these details only to read and reply to your enquiry, and to arrange a commission if you go ahead. They aren’t shared, sold or added to any mailing list. Messages stay in my email for as long as they’re useful for our conversation or your commission.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address to keep the service secure and running. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

Fonts and images

Everything on the site, including the fonts, is served from the site itself, so your browser doesn’t contact any other companies while you look around.

Your choices

You can ask me what information I hold about you, or ask me to delete it, at any time. Just send a message through the contact form. If you’re unhappy with how I’ve handled your information, you can also contact the Information Commissioner’s Office.
